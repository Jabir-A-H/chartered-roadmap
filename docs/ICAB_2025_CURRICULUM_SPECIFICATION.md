## 1. Executive Framework & Vision

The ICAB Chartered Accountancy (CA) Curriculum 2025 transforms the qualification from a traditional financial record-keeping credential into a **world-class business leadership and strategic stewardship standard**. Designed to develop future-ready, multidisciplinary financial leaders, the curriculum shifts focus from historical compliance toward **foresight, digital architecture, sustainability governance, and executive boardroom influence**.

```
                           ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                           │      C-Suite Modern Chartered Accountant     │
                           │   (Partner, CFO, CEO, Strategic ESG Leader)  │
                           └──────────────────────▲───────────────────────┘
                                                  │
                                       DESIGN DOWN METHODOLOGY

     ┌────────────────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────┐

┌────┴─────────────────────────┐  ┌─────────────────────────────┐  ┌───────────────────────────┴───┐
│     ADVANCED LEVEL (AL)      │  │   PROFESSIONAL LEVEL (PL)   │  │    CERTIFICATE LEVEL (CL)     │
│   3 Papers | 300 Marks       │  │    7 Papers | 700 Marks     │  │     7 Papers | 600 Marks      │

└──────────────────────────────┘  └─────────────────────────────┘  └───────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Design Down Methodology (DDM)
The instructional architecture of Curriculum 2025 is engineered using **Design Down Methodology**:
1. **Practitioner Endpoint Definition:** Explicitly mapping the comprehensive competencies, integrity standards, analytical rigor, and technological capabilities required of an elite C-suite Chartered Accountant.
2. **Reverse Curriculum Engineering:** Reverse-engineering educational milestones, assessment rubrics, practical articleship requirements, and software lab simulations backwards from that senior practitioner profile.

## 2. The 4 Strategic Pillars & 7 Career Capabilities

### The 4 Strategic Pillars
1. **Green Governance & Ethical Guardianship (ESG & Public Interest):**
   - Integrates sustainability disclosure (IFRS S1 & S2) and IAASB ISSA 5000 assurance standards.
   - Embeds double materiality: analyzing financial impact on the entity alongside the entity's impact on climate and society.
   - Enforces the IESBA Code of Ethics for Professional Accountants and active public interest advocacy.
2. **Strategic Shift: Compliance to Enablers (Holistic Value Creation):**
   - Transcends statutory balance-sheet reporting to drive corporate resource allocation, enterprise risk management, and strategic commercial advisory.
   - Fosters multidisciplinary collaboration between finance, operations, technology, and governance.
3. **Digital Transformation & the "ABCD" Paradigm:**
   - Deeply embeds **A**rtificial Intelligence, **B**lockchain, **C**ybersecurity, and **D**ata Analytics into daily accounting workflows.
   - Demands practical mastery of Computer-Assisted Audit Techniques (CAATs), ERP migrations, SIEM threat monitoring, and forensic data interrogation.
4. **Global Benchmark & Transnational Mobility:**
   - Fully harmonized with the International Education Standards (IES) of the International Federation of Accountants (IFAC).
   - Reciprocal benchmarking against the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales (ICAEW), Chartered Accountants Australia and New Zealand (CA ANZ), and CPA Australia.

### The 7 Definitive Career Capabilities
All 17 syllabus modules systematically cultivate seven core career competencies:
1. **Strategic Value Generation:** Maximizing operational and financial performance to surpass enterprise goals.
2. **Omnichannel Communication:** Delivering articulate, boardroom-ready analysis across oral, written, and digital channels.
3. **Data-Driven Decisioning:** Transforming unstructured corporate data into high-conviction strategic initiatives.
4. **Sustainable Integrity & Ethics:** Anchoring commercial decisions in moral courage, professional skepticism, and transparency.
5. **Advanced Problem Analysis:** Modeling multifaceted scenarios to solve layered corporate challenges.
6. **Synergistic Team Leadership:** Fostering collaboration, diversity, and operational agility across organizational teams.
7. **Digital & Technical Mastery:** Mastering modern financial technologies, programming scripts, and analytical tools.

## 3. Comprehensive 17-Examination Framework

The qualification comprises **17 examinations** totaling **1,600 marks** across three progressive tiers.

```
Total Qualification Marks: 1,600 Marks
├── Certificate Level (CL)   : 7 Exams  | 600 Marks (5x 100m, 2x 50m) | 3 Sittings/Year
├── Professional Level (PL)  : 7 Exams  | 700 Marks (7x 100m)          | 2 Sittings/Year
└── Advanced Level (AL)      : 3 Exams  | 300 Marks (3x 100m)          | 2 Sittings/Year
```

## 4. Mandatory Hands-On Practical IT Labs

Curriculum 2025 incorporates **five practical lab class syllabi** into the qualification, equipping candidates with real-world tool proficiencies. Administered independently by the ICAB IT Training Centre, these practical workshops are decoupled from written examination papers (`c7` Information Technology and `p7` IT Governance) and hosted in an autonomous sub-roadmap (`data/icab-it-labs.json`) linked via companion portals (`linkedRoadmaps`).

```
                             HANDS-ON IT LAB WORKSHOPS
                             (Sub-Roadmap: icab-it-labs)
                                         │
        ┌────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────┐
        │                                                                 │
  CERTIFICATE LEVEL (Companion to c7)                            PROFESSIONAL LEVEL (Companion to p7)
  ├── Accounting Software Implementation (2-4 wks)               ├── BI, Big Data & AI in Business (1-2 wks)
  └── Applied Excel for Accountants (1-2 wks)                    ├── IS Security & Digital Forensics (2-3 wks)
                                                                 └── Banking-Specific CAATs Scenarios (1-2 wks)
```

### 1. Accounting Software Implementation (QuickBooks / Sage / Commercial ERP)
- **Host Module:** Information Technology (`c7`) | **Duration:** 2–4 Weeks (Hands-on practical sessions)
- **Tools:** Intuit QuickBooks Desktop/Online, Sage 50, Commercial ERP sandbox.
- **Curriculum Activities:**
  1. *Requirements Analysis:* Business feasibility analysis and system specification documentation.
  2. *System Setup:* Configuring company file, currency, fiscal calendar, and multi-tier Chart of Accounts (COA).
  3. *Core Accounting Operations:* Setting up Accounts Receivable (AR), Accounts Payable (AP), General Ledger (GL), bank feeds, and statutory tax parameters.
  4. *Data Migration & Rollback:* Importing legacy master data (CSV/Excel), executing trial balance balance-checks, and designing rollback plans.
  5. *Testing & Training:* User Acceptance Testing (UAT), audit trail testing, user permission role matrix setup, and staff onboarding.
  6. *Post-Implementation Review:* Conducting Information Systems (IS) post-implementation audit.

### 2. Applied Excel for Accountants: Hands-On Techniques
- **Host Module:** Information Technology (`c7`) | **Duration:** 1–2 Weeks (Hands-on practical sessions)
- **Tools:** Microsoft Excel 365, Power Query.
- **Curriculum Activities:**
  1. *Advanced Formula Mechanics:* Dynamic array formulas, `XLOOKUP`, nested `INDEX/MATCH`, `LET`, and `LAMBDA`.
  2. *Data Hygiene & ETL:* Cleaning messy trial balances using Power Query, unpivoting columns, and removing duplicates.
  3. *Multi-Variable Modeling:* Building 3-statement interconnected financial models (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow).
  4. *Dynamic Analysis:* Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, What-If Analysis, Scenario Manager, and Goal Seek.
  5. *Executive Dashboards:* Creating visual KPI dashboards for working capital, gross margin trends, and covenant tracking.

### 3. Business Intelligence, Data Analytics & Big Data with AI Applications
- **Host Module:** IT Governance (`p7`) | **Duration:** 1–2 Weeks (Hands-on practical sessions)
- **Tools:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop, Tableau Public, Python (Pandas/NumPy), Cloud Data Warehouses (Google BigQuery / Azure SQL).
- **Curriculum Activities:**
  1. *Decision Support Modeling:* Constructing multi-criteria decision matrices with weighted scoring models.
  2. *BI Visualization Pipelines:* Connecting Power BI/Tableau to live databases; designing interactive drill-down reports and heatmaps.
  3. *Big Data Ingestion:* Handling datasets exceeding 1,000,000 transactions; optimizing queries via DirectQuery and star-schema dimensional modeling.
  4. *AI-Powered Analytics:* Applying machine learning time-series decomposition, anomaly detection, and AI automated forecasting visuals.

### 4. Information Systems Security & Forensics
- **Host Module:** IT Governance (`p7`) | **Duration:** 2–3 Weeks (Hands-on practical sessions)
- **Tools:** Microsoft Compliance Manager, AWS IAM, Azure Active Directory / Entra ID, Windows Defender ATP, pfSense, Splunk Free, ELK Stack, Autopsy Digital Forensics, FTK Imager, OpenVAS, Metasploit, Nmap, Burp Suite.
- **Curriculum Activities:**
  1. *Compliance Mapping:* Mapping ISO/IEC 27001 control objectives using compliance management software.
  2. *Cloud IAM Enforcement:* Configuring Least Privilege Access, Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), and RBAC policies in AWS/Azure.
  3. *Perimeter Security:* Designing stateful firewall rules, NAT configurations, and endpoint threat policies.
  4. *SIEM Monitoring:* Ingesting syslog data into Splunk/ELK, creating alerting dashboards for brute-force attacks and abnormal egress traffic.
  5. *Forensic Investigation:* Imaging storage media using FTK Imager, maintaining chain-of-custody documentation, and extracting artifacts using Autopsy.
  6. *Vulnerability Scanning (VAPT):* Executing authorized vulnerability assessments and generating executive remediation reports.

### 5. IS Audit Lab: Banking-Specific CAAT Scenarios (ISACA Aligned)
- **Host Module:** IT Governance (`p7`) | **Duration:** 1–2 Weeks (Hands-on practical sessions)
- **Frameworks:** Aligned with ISACA CISA guidelines and Bangladesh Bank ICT Security Guidelines.
- **Tools:** ACL Analytics, CaseWare IDEA, SQL queries, Python scripts.
- **Real-World Scenarios Executed:**
  1. *Duplicate Transaction Scanning:* Querying Core Banking System (CBS) transaction tables to detect duplicate transactions matching Date, Account Number, Amount, and Channel within narrow timestamps.
  2. *Maker-Checker Segregation Bypass:* Extracting GL journal creation and posting logs to flag instances where `Maker_ID == Checker_ID`.
  3. *Dormant Account Fraud Interrogation:* Filtering dormant account reactivation logs to flag accounts reactivated by unauthorized operators followed by high-value cash withdrawals or wire transfers within 24–48 hours.
  4. *Independent Interest Recalculation:* Re-computing daily compound interest using Python scripts and comparing results against CBS calculations to uncover systemic calculation errors or deliberate skimming.
  5. *Segregation of Duties (SOD) Matrix Conflict:* Querying CBS active user entitlements against an incompatible role matrix (e.g. Loan Origination + Loan Disbursement privileges).
  6. *Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Velocity Surges:* Analyzing 12 months of historical deposit volumes to flag accounts exhibiting >500% turnover spikes with immediate rapid structuring below the BDT 1,000,000 CTR reporting threshold.
  7. *Off-Hours / Midnight Privileged Access:* Auditing CBS login timestamps to detect privileged database administrator actions performed outside authorized shift windows.
  8. *Cheque Clearing Limit Tampering:* Auditing BACPS clearing transactions where clearing officer approval bypassed authorized transaction threshold caps.

## 5. Entry Pathways & Articleship Regulations

```
                                  FOUR ADMISSION PATHWAYS
                                             │
      ┌───────────────────┬──────────────────┴──────────────────┬───────────────────┐
      │                   │                                     │                   │
GRADUATE ROUTE      HSC / A-LEVEL                         PAS ROUTE           QUALIFIED ROUTE
(Honours/Masters)   (School Leavers)                 (Enrolled Undergrads)    (ACCA / ICMAB)
• 3-Year Articleship• 4-Year Articleship              • Exams during uni      • 2-Year Articleship
• Parallel Exams    • 3.5y for top A-Levels           • 2.5y or 2.0y Art.     • 100% CL Exempt
• University CPL    • SSC/HSC GPA 8.0                 • 50% class attendance  • Selective PL Exempt
```

### Route 1: Graduate Entry (Honours / Masters)
- **Eligibility:** Graduation or post-graduation in any discipline with at least 7 points aggregate (CGPA 3.0+ = 3 points, CGPA 2.0–2.99 = 2 points).
- **Articleship Term:** **3 Years (36 Months)** of continuous practical training at an approved CA firm.
- **PATC Requirement:** Mandatory 1-month intensive Pre-Articleship Training Course in audit fundamentals and business communication prior to commencement of firm duties.
- **Exam Progression:** Certificate Level exams can be attempted concurrently during articleship. Passing corresponding Certificate subjects unlocks Professional Level subjects.

### Route 2: HSC / A-Level Direct Entry (School Leavers)
- **Eligibility:** Minimum aggregate GPA 8.0 in SSC & HSC combined, or minimum 36 O-Level points (5–7 subjects) and 12 A-Level points (up to 3 subjects).
- **Articleship Term:** **4 Years (48 Months)**. Fast-tracked to **3.5 Years (42 Months)** for high A-Level achievers (minimum Grade A in three A-Level subjects).
- **PATC Requirement:** Mandatory 1-month course prior to starting work.

### Route 3: Pre-Articleship Student (PAS) Route (University Undergraduates)
- **Target Audience:** Enrolled undergraduate and graduate students in recognized universities.
- **Syllabus Privileges:** Candidates sit Certificate Level and Professional Level examinations **prior to joining a CA firm**.
- **Mandatory Requirement:** 50% minimum attendance in coaching classes per subject.
- **Shortened Articleship Guarantee:** Upon graduating and joining an ICAB-approved firm within 1 year of final university exams:
  - Articleship reduced to **2.5 Years (30 Months)** if all Certificate Level papers are cleared.
  - Articleship reduced to **2.0 Years (24 Months)** if all Professional Level papers are cleared.

### Route 4: Qualified Professional Route (ACCA / ICMAB Members)
- **Target Audience:** Full members and affiliates of the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) and Associate/Fellow members of ICMAB (ACMA/FCMA).
- **Articleship Term:** Shortened to **2.0 Years (24 Months)**.
- **Exemptions Granted:**
  - **100% Exemption from all 7 Certificate Level papers.**
  - **ICMAB Members:** Exempt from 3 Professional papers: *Business Strategy and Technology* (`p3`), *Corporate Laws & Practices* (`p6`), and *IT Governance* (`p7`). Sit remaining 4 PL papers.
  - **ACCA Members:** Exempt from 2 Professional papers: *Business Strategy and Technology* (`p3`) and *IT Governance* (`p7`). Additionally exempt from *Audit and Assurance* (`p1`) if ACCA *Advanced Audit and Assurance (AAA)* was completed.
  - Both groups sit all 3 Advanced Level papers (*Corporate Reporting*, *Strategic Business Management and Leadership*, and *Case Study*).

## 6. Credit for Prior Learning (CPL) University Framework

ICAB maintains bilateral Credit for Prior Learning (CPL) agreements with leading public and private universities in Bangladesh. Students graduating from accredited departments with specified minimum grades (typically B+ or 65%+) are granted paper exemptions at the Certificate Level:

- **University of Dhaka (DU):** Department of Accounting & Information Systems (AIS), Department of Finance, Department of Banking & Insurance, and Institute of Business Administration (IBA). (Up to 4–5 Certificate exemptions).
- **Jahangirnagar University (JU):** Department of Accounting & Information Systems.
- **Jagannath University (JnU):** Department of Accounting & Information Systems.
- **University of Chittagong (CU):** Department of Accounting.
- **University of Rajshahi (RU):** Department of Accounting & Information Systems.
- **Bangladesh University of Professionals (BUP):** Faculty of Business Studies.
- **Top Private Universities:** North South University (NSU), BRAC University, American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB), Independent University, Bangladesh (IUB), East West University (EWU), United International University (UIU).

## 7. Governance, Review Cycles & Examination Policy

1. **Biannual Structural Review:** ICAB conducts formal curriculum reviews every two years to ensure continuous harmonization with international benchmarks.
2. **Dynamic Taxation Updates:** Direct and indirect tax modules (*Principles of Taxation* `c5` and *Business Planning: Taxation & Compliance* `p5`) are dynamically updated annually to incorporate the National Finance Act, SROs, and judicial precedents.
3. **Examination Passing Bar:** Passing mark for all 17 examinations is **50%**.
4. **Sitting Windows:**
   - Certificate Level: Sat **3 times per year** (typically March/April, July/August, November/December).
   - Professional Level: Sat **2 times per year** (typically June and December).
   - Advanced Level: Sat **2 times per year** (typically June and December).
