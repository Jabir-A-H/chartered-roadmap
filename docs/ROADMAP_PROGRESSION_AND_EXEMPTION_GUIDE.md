## 2. Comprehensive Route Specifications

### Route 1: Graduate Entry (Honours / Masters)
- **Target Candidates:** Graduates or post-graduates in any discipline (Business, Science, Engineering, Humanities).
- **Academic Merit Criteria:**
  - Minimum **7 points** aggregate up to graduation from a recognised university.
  - At least **2 points** at each academic tier.
  - Point Scale:
    - First Division / Class or CGPA 3.00+ (out of 4.00) = **3 points**
    - Second Division / Class or CGPA 2.00–2.99 = **2 points**
    - Third Division / Class or CGPA < 2.00 = **1 point** (does not meet threshold)
- **Articleship Term:** **3 Years (36 Months)** of practical training at an ICAB-approved Chartered Accountancy firm.
- **Mandatory PATC:** 1-month full-time Pre-Articleship Training Course in Audit & Assurance and Business English prior to client engagements.
- **Exam Strategy:**
  - Sit Certificate Level exams concurrently with articleship.
  - Clear Certificate Level papers to earn eligibility for corresponding Professional Level papers.
  - Complete 3 years of articleship, clear all 17 papers, and pass Ethics modules to qualify as an **Associate Chartered Accountant (ACA)**.

---

### Route 2: HSC / A-Level Direct Entry (School Leavers)
- **Target Candidates:** Students entering directly after Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) or Cambridge/Edexcel GCE A-Levels.
- **Academic Merit Criteria:**
  - **National Curriculum:** Minimum aggregate **GPA 8.00** combined across SSC and HSC (minimum GPA 3.5 in each).
  - **English Medium (GCE):** Minimum **36 points** in O-Levels (best 5 to 7 subjects) and **12 points** in A-Levels (up to 3 subjects).
    - Point scale: `A*=6`, `A=5`, `B=4`, `C=3`, `D=2`, `E=1`.
- **Articleship Term:** **4 Years (48 Months)**.
  - *Fast-Track Option:* Reduced to **3.5 Years (42 Months)** if achieving Grade A or higher in at least three A-Level subjects with required O-Levels.
- **PATC Requirement:** Mandatory 1-month training course before firm duties.

---

### Route 3: Pre-Articleship Student (PAS) Route (University Undergraduates)
- **Target Candidates:** Currently enrolled undergraduate and postgraduate university students.
- **Core Advantage:** Candidates can study for and pass ICAB examination papers **while still studying at university**, dramatically compressing their post-graduation articleship time.
- **Registration Process:**
  1. Submit university student ID, transcripts, and recommendation letter to the ICAB PAS Wing.
  2. Register for ICAB academic coaching sessions.
  3. **Mandatory Class Attendance:** Candidates must maintain at least **50% class attendance** per module to become eligible for the examination admit card.
- **Exam Attempt Rules:**
  - Candidates may sit all 7 Certificate Level papers.
  - Candidates may sit Professional Level papers **provided** they have passed the corresponding Certificate Level prerequisite paper.
- **Articleship Term Reduction upon Graduation:**
  - Must join an approved CA firm within **1 year** of final university examinations.
  - If **Certificate Level (7 papers)** is cleared: Articleship duration reduced to **2.5 Years (30 Months)**.
  - If **Professional Level (7 papers)** is cleared: Articleship duration reduced to **2.0 Years (24 Months)**.

---

### Route 4: Qualified Professional Route (ACCA & ICMAB Members)
- **Target Candidates:** Full members/affiliates of ACCA (UK) and associate/fellow members of ICMAB (Bangladesh).
- **Articleship Term:** Reduced to **2.0 Years (24 Months)** at an approved CA firm.
- **Exemptions & Papers to Sit:**

```
QUALIFIED ROUTE ROADMAP:
├── Certificate Level (7 Papers) : 100% EXEMPT FOR BOTH ACCA & ICMAB
│
├── Professional Level (7 Papers) :
│   ├── ICMAB Members Sit (4 Papers):
│   │   ├── p1 Audit and Assurance
│   │   ├── p2 Financial Accounting and Reporting
│   │   ├── p4 Financial Management
│   │   └── p5 Business Planning: Taxation & Compliance
│   │   [EXEMPT: p3 Business Strategy, p6 Corporate Laws, p7 IT Governance]
│   │
│   └── ACCA Members Sit (4 or 5 Papers):
│       ├── p1 Audit and Assurance (EXEMPT if ACCA AAA Advanced Audit was passed)
│       ├── p2 Financial Accounting and Reporting
│       ├── p4 Financial Management
│       ├── p5 Business Planning: Taxation & Compliance
│       └── p6 Corporate Laws & Practices
│       [EXEMPT: p3 Business Strategy, p7 IT Governance]
│
└── Advanced Level (3 Papers) : ALL CANDIDATES MUST SIT ALL 3 PAPERS
    ├── v1 Corporate Reporting
    ├── v2 Strategic Business Management and Leadership
    └── v3 Case Study
```

| **University of Dhaka (DU)** — Finance / Banking | Up to 3–4 Papers | `c2` Accounting, `c3` BTF, `c4` Management Information |
| **University of Dhaka (DU)** — IBA (BBA/MBA) | Up to 3–4 Papers | `c2` Accounting, `c3` BTF, `c4` Management Information |

| **Jagannath University (JnU)** — AIS Department | Up to 4 Papers | `c1` Assurance, `c2` Accounting, `c3` BTF, `c4` Mgmt Info |
| **University of Chittagong (CU)** — Accounting | Up to 4 Papers | `c1` Assurance, `c2` Accounting, `c3` BTF, `c4` Mgmt Info |
| **University of Rajshahi (RU)** — AIS Department | Up to 4 Papers | `c1` Assurance, `c2` Accounting, `c3` BTF, `c4` Mgmt Info |

*Note: Principles of Taxation (`c5`) and Information Technology (`c7`) typically must be sat by all candidates unless specific localized curriculum alignment is certified.*

## 5. Examination Sitting Strategy & Progression Prerequisites

1. **Certificate Level Flexibility:**
   - 3 exam sessions per calendar year.
   - Candidates may sit any number of papers (from 1 to all 7) in any sitting.
   - Recommended pairing: Pair quantitative papers (`c2` Accounting, `c4` MI) with law or theory papers (`c6` BL, `c1` Assurance).
2. **Professional Level Prerequisites:**
   - A candidate is eligible to attempt a Professional Level subject if they have passed all 7 Certificate Level papers **OR** have passed the specific corresponding Certificate paper:
     - Clear `c2` (Accounting) $\rightarrow$ Unlocks `p2` (Financial Accounting & Reporting).
     - Clear `c1` (Assurance) $\rightarrow$ Unlocks `p1` (Audit & Assurance).
     - Clear `c3` (BTF) / `c4` (MI) $\rightarrow$ Unlocks `p3` (BST) and `p4` (FM).
     - Clear `c5` (Taxation) $\rightarrow$ Unlocks `p5` (Business Planning: Tax).
     - Clear `c6` (Business Laws) $\rightarrow$ Unlocks `p6` (Corporate Laws).
     - Clear `c7` (IT) $\rightarrow$ Unlocks `p7` (IT Governance).
3. **Advanced Level Gateway:**
   - All 7 Professional Level papers must be successfully cleared before attempting any Advanced Level examination.
   - The capstone *Case Study* (`v3`) is typically sat in the final year of articleship or after clearing `v1` and `v2`.

## 6. Steps to ACA Membership

```
    CLEAR ALL 17 EXAMS
          │
          ▼
    COMPLETE PRESCRIBED ARTICLESHIP (2, 2.5, 3, or 4 Years)
          │
          ▼
    SUBMIT PRACTICAL TRAINING LOGBOOK & PRINCIPAL'S CERTIFICATE
          │
          ▼
    COMPLETE MANDATORY PROFESSIONAL ETHICS & CPD ONBOARDING
          │
          ▼
    ADMISSION TO ICAB AS ASSOCIATE CHARTERED ACCOUNTANT (ACA)
```

Upon formal admission as an ACA:
- Entitled to use the designatory letters **ACA**.
- Eligible to apply for ICAEW membership under the reciprocal pathway agreement.
- Eligible to advance to **FCA (Fellow Chartered Accountant)** after 5 years of post-qualification standing.
