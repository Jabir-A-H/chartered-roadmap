## Executive Summary & Statutory Triad of Bangladesh

Under the legislative architecture of the People's Republic of Bangladesh, the professional accounting, assurance, and corporate governance ecosystem is anchored by **three autonomous statutory chartered bodies** created by Acts of Parliament under the administrative jurisdiction of the Ministry of Commerce:

1. **The Institute of Chartered Accountants of Bangladesh (ICAB):** Established under the *Bangladesh Chartered Accountants Order, 1973 (President's Order No. 2 of 1973)*. Holds the exclusive statutory monopoly over the independent financial audit of statutory entities, corporations, and non-profits under Section 210 of the *Companies Act, 1994*.
2. **The Institute of Cost and Management Accountants of Bangladesh (ICMAB):** Re-enacted and modernized under the *Cost and Management Accountants Act, 2018 (Act No. LXX of 2018)*, repealing the 1977 Ordinance. Holds the statutory monopoly over cost audit, cost accounting record certifications, inventory valuation, and enterprise management accounting under Section 220 of the *Companies Act, 1994* and the *Cost Audit (Report) Rules, 1997*.
3. **The Institute of Chartered Secretaries of Bangladesh (ICSB):** Established under the *Chartered Secretaries Act, 2010 (Act No. 25 of 2010)*. Governs corporate compliance, board administration, company secretarial practice, and holds the exclusive mandate for Secretarial Audit and Corporate Governance Compliance Certification under the *BSEC Corporate Governance Code (CGC 2018)*.

Together with global benchmark credentials (**ACCA, CIMA/CGMA, CMA US, and ICAEW**), these bodies form an interconnected grid of corporate leadership, assurance, and public accountability.

## SECTION 1: ICMAB (Cost and Management Accountants of Bangladesh)

### 1.1 Governance & Statutory Framework
- **Parent Body:** Institute of Cost and Management Accountants of Bangladesh.
- **Official Website:** [https://www.icmab.gov.bd](https://www.icmab.gov.bd)
- **Legal Foundation:** The **Cost and Management Accountants Act, 2018 (২০১৮ সনের ৭০ নং আইন)** received presidential assent on 14 November 2018, repealing and modernizing the *Cost and Management Accountants Ordinance, 1977 (Ordinance No. LIII of 1977)*.
- **Regulatory Administration:** Operates as a statutory body corporate under the administrative control of the Ministry of Commerce, Government of Bangladesh.
- **Affiliations & Recognitions:**
  - Full Member of the International Federation of Accountants (**IFAC**).
  - Member of the Confederation of Asian and Pacific Accountants (**CAPA**).
  - Member of the South Asian Federation of Accountants (**SAFA**).

### 1.2 Statutory Mandates & Cost Audit Authority
Under Bangladesh law, ICMAB members possess exclusive statutory authority across critical public-interest domains:
1. **Statutory Cost Audit (Companies Act 1994, Section 220):**
   - The Government of Bangladesh possesses the power under Section 220 of the Companies Act 1994 to mandate that an audit of cost accounts be conducted for companies engaged in production, processing, manufacturing, or mining activities.
   - Section 220(2) specifically mandates that the auditor conducting the cost audit must be a **Cost and Management Accountant** within the meaning of the Cost and Management Accountants Act.
2. **Cost Audit (Report) Rules, 1997:**
   - Outlines the statutory format, questionnaire, reconciliation tables, and submission protocol for cost audit reports. The cost auditor must submit the report to the Government and the company's Board of Directors.
3. **Mandatory Cost Audit Gazette Notifications (Ministry of Commerce):**
   - The Ministry of Commerce has gazetted mandatory cost audits across key industrial sectors, including:
     - Pharmaceuticals & Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs)
     - Fertilizers (Chemical, Urea, TSP)
     - Sugar mills (public and private)
     - Textile spinning and composite mills
     - Cement manufacturing companies
     - Petroleum refining, lubricants, and LPG distribution
     - Natural gas extraction and transmission
     - Power generation and distribution enterprises
     - Edible oil refining and vegetable ghee
4. **Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission (BSEC) IPO Rules:**
   - BSEC Public Issue Rules require certified cost audits and verification of manufacturing costs, plant capacity utilization, and raw material reconciliation by certified CMAs before public offering approvals.
5. **Quality Assurance Board (QAB):**
   - ICMAB maintains an independent Quality Assurance Board to monitor the audit files, work papers, and ethical compliance of practicing CMA firms.

#### Detailed Breakdown of ICMAB Examination Papers

##### Level 1: Foundation Level (Total: 400 Marks)
1. **FR 111: Fundamentals of Financial Accounting (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Double-entry bookkeeping system, accounting cycle, ledger mechanics.
   - Adjusting entries, bank reconciliation, trial balance, depreciation accounting.
   - Preparation of financial statements for sole traders, non-trading entities, and partnerships under IFRS/IAS.
2. **TA 112: Business Quantitative Analysis (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Business mathematics: Time value of money, annuities, loan amortization, matrices, differential and integral calculus for business optimization.
   - Business statistics: Frequency distributions, measures of central tendency and dispersion, probability distributions (Binomial, Poisson, Normal), hypothesis testing, index numbers, and time-series forecasting.
3. **EF 113: Business Economics & International Business (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Microeconomics: Demand-supply elasticity, consumer utility, production functions, cost behavior, market structures (perfect competition, monopoly, oligopoly).
   - Macroeconomics: National income accounting, monetary and fiscal policy, inflation, banking system of Bangladesh, foreign exchange rates, Balance of Payments (BoP), WTO and regional trade pacts.
4. **MS 114: Fundamentals of Management & Business Communication (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Principles of management, organizational theory, planning, organizing, staffing, leading, and control.
   - Business communication: Communication process, non-verbal communication, report writing, executive summaries, business presentations, professional ethics.

##### Level 2: Intermediate Level I (Total: 400 Marks)
1. **CM 121: Cost Accounting (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Cost terminology, classifications, and cost sheet design.
   - Material costing: EOQ, JIT, inventory valuation (FIFO, Weighted Average), material control procedures.
   - Labor costing: Timekeeping, payroll systems, incentive schemes, labor turnover.
   - Overhead costing: Allocation, apportionment, secondary distribution, absorption costing rates under BCAS 4 & 5.
   - Costing methods: Job costing, batch costing, contract costing, process costing (normal/abnormal losses, equivalent production units, FIFO vs. weighted average).
2. **FR 222: Intermediate Financial Accounting (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Framework for financial reporting under IFRS.
   - Revenue recognition (IFRS 15), leases (IFRS 16), property, plant and equipment (IAS 16), intangible assets (IAS 38), impairment (IAS 36), inventories (IAS 2).
   - Preparation of corporate financial statements: Statement of Financial Position, Statement of Profit or Loss, Statement of Changes in Equity, Statement of Cash Flows (IAS 7 direct and indirect methods).
3. **TA 223: Information & Communication Technologies (50 Marks | 2 Hours)**
   - Computer hardware, systems software, database management systems (DBMS), relational databases, SQL queries.
   - Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems: SAP, Oracle, cloud architectures, data analytics tools, cybersecurity, IT audit controls.
4. **MS 224: Marketing & Supply Chain Management (50 Marks | 2 Hours)**
   - Marketing mix (4Ps/7Ps), customer segmentation, targeting, positioning (STP), product lifecycle.
   - Supply chain management: Procurement, logistics, warehousing, distribution networks, supply chain risk management.
5. **LT 125: Corporate & Business Laws (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Companies Act, 1994: Company incorporation, Memorandum & Articles of Association, share capital, prospectuses, management and administration, directors' duties, corporate winding-up.
   - Business laws: Contract Act 1872, Sale of Goods Act 1930, Negotiable Instruments Act 1881, Partnership Act 1932, Bangladesh Labor Act 2006.

##### Level 3: Intermediate Level II (Total: 400 Marks)
1. **CM 231: Management Accounting (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Marginal (variable) costing vs. absorption costing; cost-volume-profit (CVP) analysis, break-even point, margin of safety, multi-product CVP.
   - Short-term decision making: Relevant costs, make-or-buy decisions, special orders, closing unprofitable segments, pricing decisions.
   - Budgeting and budgetary control: Master budget, flexible budgeting, zero-based budgeting (ZBB), activity-based budgeting (ABB).
   - Standard costing: Material, labor, and overhead variance analysis.
2. **EF 232: Financial Management (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Financial statement analysis: Liquidity, solvency, profitability, efficiency ratios, DuPont decomposition.
   - Working capital management: Cash management, receivables management (credit terms, factoring), inventory optimization.
   - Capital budgeting: Discounted cash flow (DCF), Net Present Value (NPV), Internal Rate of Return (IRR), Modified IRR (MIRR), Payback, capital rationing.
   - Cost of capital: WACC, cost of equity (CAPM, Dividend Discount Model), cost of debt, capital structure theories (MM proposition, pecking order theory).
3. **AA 133: Auditing (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - International Standards on Auditing (ISA) adopted in Bangladesh (BSA).
   - Audit planning, risk assessment, internal control evaluation, test of controls, substantive procedures, audit sampling.
   - Audit evidence, analytical procedures, audit documentation.
   - Audit reports: Unmodified opinion, qualified opinion, adverse opinion, disclaimer of opinion, Emphasis of Matter, Key Audit Matters (KAM).
4. **LT 234: Fundamentals of Tax, VAT & Public Financial Management (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Income Tax Act, 2023: Scope of total income, heads of income (salaries, income from business, capital gains, other sources), tax depreciation rules, minimum tax.
   - Value Added Tax and Supplementary Duty Act, 2012: Registration, VAT calculation methods, input tax credit, VAT invoices (Mushak 6.3), withholding VAT (VDS), returns filing.
   - Public Financial Management (PFM): National budget cycle, public procurement rules (PPA 2006 / PPR 2008), government accounting procedures.

##### Level 4: Advanced Level I (Total: 400 Marks)
1. **CM 341: Strategic Cost and Management Accounting (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Activity-Based Costing (ABC) and Activity-Based Management (ABM).
   - Target costing, life-cycle costing, Kaizen costing, value chain analysis.
   - Theory of Constraints (TOC) and throughput accounting.
   - Strategic pricing, quality costing (BCAS 29), environmental and carbon accounting.
   - Performance evaluation: Balanced Scorecard, Economic Value Added (EVA®), transfer pricing across multinational divisions (BCAS 16).
2. **FR 342: Advanced Financial Reporting (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Group accounting and consolidated financial statements (IFRS 10, IFRS 3 Business Combinations, IAS 28 Associates & Joint Ventures).
   - Financial instruments (IFRS 9 classification, measurement, hedge accounting, impairment under ECL model).
   - Employee benefits (IAS 19 actuarial valuations), foreign currency transactions (IAS 21), deferred tax (IAS 12).
   - Sustainability reporting and ISSB Standards (IFRS S1 & IFRS S2).
3. **EF 343: Corporate Finance Strategy & Financial Market (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Mergers and acquisitions (M&A): Valuation methodologies (DCF, comparable multiples, asset-based), transaction financing, post-merger integration.
   - Corporate financial restructuring, debt refinancing, distressed asset management.
   - Financial markets: Money market instruments, bond markets, equity issuance (IPO, Rights Issue), financial derivatives (options, futures, swaps, currency hedging).
4. **LT 344: Corporate Governance & Secretarial Practices (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Theories and frameworks of corporate governance (OECD Principles, Cadbury Report).
   - BSEC Corporate Governance Code (CGC 2018): Board structures, Independent Directors, Audit Committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC).
   - Corporate secretarial practice: Board meeting procedures, drafting resolutions, AGM/EGM notices, compliance reporting, RJSC statutory filings.

##### Level 5: Advanced Level II (Total: 400 Marks)
1. **MS 351: Business Strategy (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Strategic management process: Environmental scanning, internal resource-based view (VRIO framework), SWOT, Porter's Five Forces, PESTEL analysis.
   - Strategy formulation: Corporate-level strategies, business-level competitive strategies (cost leadership, differentiation, focus), international expansion strategies.
   - Strategy execution, balanced scorecard alignment, change management, corporate turnaround strategies.
2. **AA 252: Cost & Management Audit (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Statutory cost audit legal framework: Companies Act 1994 Sec 220, Cost Audit (Report) Rules 1997.
   - Application of Bangladesh Cost Accounting Standards (BCAS 1–31) in audit engagements.
   - Cost audit planning, inventory valuation audits, manufacturing overhead verification, capacity utilization testing (BCAS 28), power and fuel consumption ratios.
   - Drafting cost audit reports, reporting irregularities to the Ministry of Commerce, management audit, operational audit, environmental and energy audits.
3. **LT 453: Advanced Income Tax & VAT (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Corporate taxation: Corporate tax computation for listed, non-listed, banking, and manufacturing entities; double taxation avoidance agreements (DTAA).
   - Transfer pricing regulations in Bangladesh: Arm's length principle, transfer pricing documentation, Country-by-Country (CbC) reporting.
   - Advanced VAT: VAT audit procedures, appellate tribunals, ADR (Alternative Dispute Resolution), high court references.
4. **CS 154: Integrated Case Study (100 Marks | 3 Hours)**
   - Comprehensive multi-disciplinary business case study integrating strategic cost management, advanced financial reporting, corporate finance, taxation, ethics, and corporate governance.
   - Evaluation of strategic dilemmas, risk-return trade-offs, financial modeling, and Board-level recommendations.

### 1.5 Practical Experience, Articleship & Membership Regulations

To earn the **ACMA (Associate Cost and Management Accountant)** designation and full membership, candidates must satisfy both examination and practical training requirements:

1. **CMA Articleship Policy:**
   - **Duration:** **12 Months** of structured articleship.
   - **Supervision:** Must be completed under the direct supervision of an active professional member (**ACMA or FCMA**) of ICMAB.
   - **Approved Venues:** Practicing CMA firms, recognized public/private limited corporations, multinational corporations, commercial banks, financial institutions, or statutory government enterprises.
   - **Alternative Experience Recognition:** Candidates with **at least 1 to 2 years** of verified relevant professional working experience in accounting, cost management, audit, or financial management within an approved organization can submit their employment records and obtain an *Articleship Completion Certificate*.
2. **Fellowship (FCMA):**
   - Minimum of **5 years** of post-qualification full-time senior management accounting, financial control, or professional practice experience as an ACMA.
3. **Continuing Professional Development (CPD):**
   - Active members must accumulate mandatory annual CPD credits through ICMAB conferences, technical workshops, seminars, and academic research to maintain their active membership and Certificate of Practice.

## SECTION 2: ICSB (Institute of Chartered Secretaries of Bangladesh)

### 2.1 Governance & Statutory Framework
- **Parent Body:** Institute of Chartered Secretaries of Bangladesh.
- **Official Website:** [https://www.icsb.edu.bd](https://www.icsb.edu.bd)
- **Legal Foundation:** The **Chartered Secretaries Act, 2010 (২০১০ সনের ২৫ নং আইন)** was enacted by Parliament on 16 June 2010 to regulate, promote, and develop the profession of Chartered Secretaries in Bangladesh.
- **Rules & Regulations:** Governed by the *Chartered Secretaries Regulations, 2011*.
- **Regulatory Administration:** Operates as a statutory body under the administrative supervision of the Ministry of Commerce, Government of Bangladesh.
- **Global Affiliations:** Full Member of the Corporate Secretaries International Association (**CSIA**).

### 2.2 Statutory Mandates & Corporate Governance Monopoly
Chartered Secretaries serve as the highest-ranking governance and compliance officers in corporate enterprises:
1. **Mandatory Appointment of Company Secretary (BSEC Regulations):**
   - Under the Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission (BSEC) regulations and Listing Rules of the Dhaka Stock Exchange (DSE) and Chittagong Stock Exchange (CSE), **every listed company is legally required to appoint a qualified Company Secretary**.
   - Condition 3 of the BSEC Corporate Governance Code (CGC 2018) specifies that the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) / Managing Director (MD), Company Secretary (CS), and Chief Financial Officer (CFO) **must be three distinct individuals**.
2. **Corporate Governance Compliance Certificate (Condition 1(5)(xxvii) of CGC 2018):**
   - Listed companies must annually obtain a compliance certificate from an independent practicing professional—specifically a **Chartered Secretary in Practice (FCS/ACS)** or a practicing Chartered Accountant—confirming adherence to the BSEC Corporate Governance Code.
   - The compliance certificate, along with a detailed compliance report (Annexure-C checklist), must be published in the Annual Report.
3. **Secretarial Audit:**
   - A comprehensive compliance audit assessing the company's operational adherence to:
     - The Companies Act, 1994
     - Securities and Exchange Ordinance, 1969 & BSEC Act, 1993
     - Listing Regulations of DSE and CSE
     - Depository Act, 1999 & Central Depository Bangladesh Ltd (CDBL) By-laws
     - Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1947
     - Bangladesh Secretarial Standards (BSS)
4. **Certificate of Practice (CoP):**
   - Members in private practice obtain a CoP to establish Chartered Secretarial firms, provide secretarial audits, issue corporate compliance certificates, and represent corporate clients before the Registrar of Joint Stock Companies and Firms (RJSC), BSEC, and courts.

### 2.3 Bangladesh Secretarial Standards (BSS)
The Secretarial Standards Board (SSB) of ICSB has formulated and issued six authoritative **Bangladesh Secretarial Standards (BSS)**:

| Standard Code | Standard Title | Key Regulatory Scope & Governance Principles |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **BSS-1** | **Meetings of the Board of Directors** | Convenes, schedules, and conducts board meetings. Minimum 4 meetings annually (at least one per quarter); 7 days written notice with agenda; quorum determination; director attendance tracking; committee meetings (Audit Committee, NRC). |
| **BSS-2** | **General Meetings (AGM & EGM)** | Convening and conducting Annual General Meetings (AGM) and Extraordinary General Meetings (EGM). Notice timelines (21 days for AGM); explanatory statements; quorum; proxy appointment (Form M); postal and electronic voting; poll procedures. |
| **BSS-3** | **Minutes** | Mandatory protocols for recording, circulating, signing, and archiving minutes of Board, Committee, and General meetings. Drafting within 15 days; entry in bound books; safe custody; permanent preservation. |
| **BSS-4** | **Dividend** | Declaration, approval, and disbursement of interim and final dividends. Timelines for fund segregation; 30-day payout window; handling unclaimed dividends; transfer to BSEC Capital Market Stabilization Fund (CMSF). |
| **BSS-5** | **Meetings Through Electronic Modes** | Technical and procedural standards for virtual and hybrid meetings (video conferencing, secure audio-visual platforms). Roll-call attendance; identity verification; encrypted electronic voting; digital archive preservation. |
| **BSS-6** | **Resolutions by Circulation** | Standards for passing urgent board resolutions without a physical meeting. Circulation of draft resolutions to all directors; required majority approval; mandatory minuting at the immediately subsequent board meeting. |

### 2.5 Practical Training & Membership Regulations (ACS / FCS)

To obtain membership of the Institute of Chartered Secretaries of Bangladesh, candidates must fulfill:
1. **Associate Membership (ACS):**
   - Successfully pass all examination levels (or approved exemptions).
   - Complete the mandatory Secretarial Internship Program / Case Study submission.
   - Possess at least **3 years of relevant professional working experience** in company secretarial, legal, finance, corporate administration, or regulatory affairs.
   - Demonstrate "fit and proper person" character standing.
2. **Fellowship Membership (FCS):**
   - Must be an active ACS member.
   - Accumulate at least **8 years of verified post-qualification leadership experience** in a senior corporate position, such as Company Secretary, Head of Legal & Compliance, or Director of Corporate Affairs.
3. **Certificate of Practice (CoP):**
   - Conferred upon ACS/FCS members who wish to engage in public practice as *Chartered Secretaries in Practice*. Entitles the practitioner to perform statutory Secretarial Audits, issue Corporate Governance compliance certificates, and provide corporate legal consultancy.
4. **Mandatory CPD Hours:**
   - Practicing and non-practicing members must earn prescribed Continuing Professional Development (CPD) hours through ICSB workshops, national conventions, and webinars.

### 3.2 Key Reciprocal Agreements & Fast-Track Pathways

#### Pathway 1: ICMAB (CMA) $\longleftrightarrow$ ICAB (CA)
* **The Landmark ICAB Credit for Prior Learning (CPL) Policy for ICMAB Members:**
  - Qualified **ACMA or FCMA** members of ICMAB receive exemptions for **10 papers out of 17** in the ICAB Chartered Accountancy curriculum.
  - **Exemptions Granted:**
    - **Certificate Level (All 7 Papers Exempted):**
      1. Assurance
      2. Accounting
      3. Business, Technology and Finance
      4. Management Information
      5. Principle of Taxation
      6. Business Law
      7. Information Technology
    - **Professional Level (3 Papers Exempted out of 7):**
      8. Business Strategy and Technology (BST)
      9. Corporate Laws & Practices
      10. IT Governance
  - **Remaining Papers to Pass (7 Papers):**
    - Professional Level: Financial Accounting & Reporting, Financial Management, Audit & Assurance, Business Planning: Taxation & Compliance (4 papers).
    - Advanced Level: Corporate Reporting, Strategic Business Management & Leadership, Case Study (3 papers).
  - **Articleship Benefit:** Mandatory CA articleship is reduced from 3 years to **2 years** for qualified ICMAB members.

#### Pathway 2: ICMAB (CMA) $\longleftrightarrow$ CIMA / CGMA (UK/USA)
* **CIMA Management Accountant Gateway Route:**
  - Qualified members and passed finalists of ICMAB receive **11 paper exemptions** spanning the CIMA Certificate in Business Accounting, Operational Level, and Management Level.
  - **The Gateway Exam:** Candidates sit only **one exam**—the **Management Case Study (MCS) Exam / CIMA Gateway Exam**.
  - **Progression to Strategic Level:** Passing the Gateway Exam awards the CIMA Advanced Diploma in Management Accounting and grants direct entry to the **Strategic Level** (E3, P3, F3 Objective Tests + Strategic Case Study Exam) to earn the ACMA and CGMA designations.
  - **Alternative Digital Route:** ICMAB members can enroll in the **CIMA Finance Leadership Program (FLP)**, validating competencies via self-paced digital continuous assessment and sitting only the Strategic Case Study (SCS).

#### Pathway 3: ICMAB (CMA) $\longleftrightarrow$ ACCA (UK / Global)
* **ICMAB Members Entering ACCA:**
  - Receive maximum exemptions (**9 papers**):
    - Applied Knowledge Level: Business and Technology (BT), Management Accounting (MA), Financial Accounting (FA).
    - Applied Skills Level: Performance Management (PM), Taxation (TX), Financial Reporting (FR), Audit and Assurance (AA), Financial Management (FM), Corporate and Business Law (LW).
  - **Remaining to Complete:** Only the **Strategic Professional Level** (Strategic Business Leader [SBL], Strategic Business Reporting [SBR], and 2 optional papers from AFM, APM, ATX, AAA) + Ethics and Professional Skills Module (EPSM).
* **ACCA Members Entering ICMAB:**
  - Receive automatic waiver from the Foundation Level and 4 paper exemptions in the Business Level (excluding local corporate law GE 05), with fast-track progression through Intermediate Levels.

#### Pathway 4: ICAB (CA) $\longleftrightarrow$ ICAEW (UK)
* **ICAEW Pathways to Membership:**
  - Qualified ICAB members with **at least 5 years of post-qualification full membership** in good standing can apply for direct ICAEW ACA membership through the *Pathways to Membership* scheme by submitting an Examination of Experience portfolio without sitting additional examinations.
* **ICAEW Examination Top-Up Route:**
  - ICAB members with less than 5 years post-qualification can achieve the ACA designation by passing only **three advanced exams**:
    1. Corporate Reporting
    2. Strategic Business Management
    3. Case Study
  - Complete the online ICAEW Ethics Learning Programme.

#### Pathway 5: Professional Accounting Bodies $\longrightarrow$ ICSB (Chartered Secretary)
* **Foundational Standing:**
  - Members of ICAB, ICMAB, ACCA, and CMA (US) receive a **100% full waiver from the ICSB Foundation Level (4 papers)**.
* **Certificate Level Exemptions:**
  - Evaluated on a paper-to-paper basis:
    - Subject Code 103 (Corporate Accounting & Valuation) — **Exempted** for CA, CMA, and ACCA holders.
    - Subject Code 202 (Direct & Indirect Tax Laws) — **Exempted** for CA and CMA holders who have completed local tax papers.
    - Subject Code 303 (Interpreting Financial & Accounting Information) — **Exempted** for CA, CMA, and ACCA holders.
* **The Non-Exemptible Statutory CS Core:**
  - To safeguard the statutory integrity of the Chartered Secretarial profession under the *Chartered Secretaries Act 2010*, the following papers are **strictly non-exemptible**:
    - Subject Code 401: Advanced Company Law & Corporate Restructuring
    - Subject Code 403: Corporate Governance & Risk Management
    - Subject Code 501: Corporate Secretaryship
    - Subject Code 503: Securities Laws & Capital Market
    - Subject Code 504: Compliance Management, Secretarial Audit & Due Diligence
